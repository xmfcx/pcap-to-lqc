Output conventions

XYZ stays in the sensor frame. Raw intensity is preserved. Coincident returns are merged and distinct recorded ranges are numbered nearest first. In LQC 1.1, all points participate in PCDA and channels map to laser IDs 0–31.

No mounting, trajectory, optical-center, or spot correction is applied. GPS week crossings and mixed sensor captures are rejected.
